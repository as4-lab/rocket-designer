# 🚀 AeroForge

AeroForge is a rocket design and learning platform built to make rocketry more accessible to beginners.

The goal is simple: **learn how rockets work, compare the costs of different components, and understand how to design the systems that make a rocket fly.**

## What AeroForge Does

* 🚀 **Rocket Design** — Explore the major components and systems that go into a rocket.
* 💰 **Price Comparison** — Compare component options and their prices to find practical alternatives.
* 💻 **Flight Computer** — Learn how a flight computer works and how its different systems fit together.
* 📚 **Step-by-Step Guides** — Break complex concepts into understandable steps instead of assuming advanced engineering knowledge.
* 🧠 **Learn as You Build** — Understand the reasoning behind each design decision, not just what to build.

## Why AeroForge?

Rocketry can be intimidating when you're starting out. There are countless components, technical concepts, and design decisions to understand before you even know where to begin.

AeroForge aims to bring those ideas together in one place and make the learning process clearer, more practical, and easier to follow.

## Project Status

🚧 **AeroForge is currently a work in progress.**

The project is being developed as an educational and experimental platform, with new features and guides being added over time.
