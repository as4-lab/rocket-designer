About AeroForge

An educational rocket-design platform: design a rocket, see the engineering consequences immediately, and learn the reasoning behind them.

You design it

AeroForge never silently changes your rocket. It analyses what you built, flags what might not work, and explains why — the decision stays yours.

Explain, don't just fail

Every warning says what the problem is, why it matters physically, and what kind of change would address it.

Honest numbers

Calculations use real engineering relationships, simplified where that keeps things understandable. Every simplification is written down.

Yours, locally

No account, no sign-up. Projects and progress are stored in your own browser.

What the simulator does and doesn't model

Included: shaped thrust curve scaled to the motor's rated impulse, drag with an altitude-dependent air density, mass loss as propellant burns, and parachute descent. Not included: wind during ascent, angle of attack, transonic drag rise, fin flutter, and the exact measured thrust trace of your specific motor. Stability uses a simplified nose-and-fins method, not full Barrowman. Treat results as estimates for comparing designs, not flight predictions.

How the numbers work

Flight: every 10 ms the simulator adds up thrust, weight and drag, divides by the current mass to get acceleration, and steps speed and height forward.

Drag: ½ × air density × speed² × drag coefficient × frontal area, with density falling off with altitude.

Stability: the distance between the centre of gravity and a simplified centre of pressure, expressed in body diameters.

Flight computer: battery life is capacity divided by total current draw, and the pyro pulse sags the voltage by current times the battery’s internal resistance.

Safety first

AeroForge is a design and learning tool. It does not replace a launch safety code. Use only commercially made motors, never build your own propellant, fly in an open area, and follow your local rocketry club’s rules and your country’s laws. Simulated results are estimates, not guarantees.

Questions

Can the assistant really do anything?

It can do anything the app supports: change any design setting, take the rocket apart, zoom into parts, rotate the view, run the simulation, save, and switch pages. It also answers rocketry questions. Anything outside the app it will tell you it cannot do.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

Projects and progress stay in your own browser. Only the text you type to the assistant is sent for it to answer.

How accurate is the simulation?

It is good for comparing designs. It leaves out wind during ascent, angle of attack, transonic drag and fin flutter, so real flights will differ.

Why is my rocket flagged as unstable?

The centre of pressure is too close to, or ahead of, the centre of gravity. Add fin area, sweep the fins back or move weight forward, then check the margin again.

Why does the flight computer need a barometer?

It is how the computer knows when the rocket has stopped climbing. Without it, the parachute never fires.
